rythm — Privacy Policy

Effective for version 1.0.0 · Last updated August 12, 2026

rythm is a wellness and performance platform. This policy explains what we collect, why, and — most importantly — where your data lives. The short version: your physiology stays on your device by default. We do not sell, rent, or trade your health data, and we never have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details — name, email, and (if you sign in with Google) your profile picture, only to identify you.
  • Profile & physiology — date of birth, sex, weight, height and training goal, which feed the recovery, strain, VO₂max and AI-metric engines.
  • Health metrics — heart rate, HRV, resting HR, steps, sleep, SpO₂, skin temperature, and workout GPS tracks you record or connect.
  • Phone number (optional) — used only to match friends from your contacts. It is never shown publicly and never used for marketing.

2. Where your data lives

By default, all of your data is stored locally on your device (in your browser or the app's local storage) and never leaves it. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly opt in:

  • Cloud sync — an opt-in switch in Settings. When you turn it on, your metrics, workouts and habits are backed up to your own Supabase project, protected by row-level security so only your account can read them. It's off until you flip it on.
  • AI features — the AI coach and score reviews may send a text summary of your physiological context (recovery, sleep, strain, habits) to a model provider to generate a reply. This happens per-request and only for the features you use.

GPS routes are stored only on your device. When you sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in itself.

Our commitment. Your health data is yours. We process it only to run the features you ask for, we never monetize it, and we give you one-tap export and one-tap delete from Settings → Data.

3. Export & delete

  • Export — download all of your data anytime (Settings → Data → Export) as JSON and CSV.
  • Delete — delete your account and all stored data, including cloud rows, anytime (Settings → Data → Delete account).
  • Clearing the browser — because data is local by default, clearing this site's storage also removes it.

4. Security

Authentication runs through Supabase Auth (Google sign-in is verified server-side), sync tables are protected by row-level security so each user can only touch their own rows, and server-side API keys never ship in the app bundle. No security system is perfect — we keep attack surface small precisely because your data is local-first.

5. Your rights & control

  • Access & portability — export your data anytime (above).
  • Deletion — delete your account and all stored data anytime (above).
  • Correction — edit your profile and metrics in Settings.
  • Consent — every data path beyond local storage (cloud sync, AI, contacts matching) is opt-in, never default.

6. Not medical advice

rythm is for training and wellness awareness, not diagnosis or treatment of any medical condition. Consult a qualified professional for medical decisions.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Use Settings → Send feedback in the app, or email privacy@rythm.app.
